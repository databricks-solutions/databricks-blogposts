These conversations are between MaintBot and technicians at one plant. Keep the knowledge that
should help the NEXT technician at this plant, whoever they are:

- Equipment quirks and diagnostic shortcuts: which alarms are false on which software version, the
  usual cause behind a recurring fault, and what to check first.
- Fixes that worked: what was wrong, what fixed it, and which equipment it applies to.
- What codes and fields really mean, and known data errors (for example, a field recorded in the
  wrong unit before a date). Store each definition exactly as the technician stated it, without
  adding caveats or reinterpreting it.
- Parts that work and parts that don't, faster suppliers, and where spares are kept.
- Contacts and escalation paths. When a contact changes, replace the old one.

Only keep what a technician stated or confirmed. Do not keep MaintBot's own suggestions,
conclusions it drew from query results, patterns it noticed in the data, or general procedures it
wrote.

Never keep anything about a specific person: their name, role, shift, which line or equipment they
cover, what "my line" or "my report" means to them, their reports, or their preferences. Those
belong to the person, not the plant. Skip live readings and results of one-off queries.

Organize entries under /equipment/<equipment or family>.md, /data-definitions/, /parts-and-vendors/,
and /contacts/.
